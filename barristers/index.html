Barristers

Each barrister at Resolution Chambers shares a common goal – to create a collegiate and unified barristers’ chambers where success is jointly earned.

Active advice

We each believe that execution and action are key to effective litigation and dispute resolution. Advice without implementation is poor advice. At Resolution Chambers you will not simply receive passive advice. You will enjoy active advice and support to address and resolve the challenges you face.

Our barristers enjoy a forceful drive to succeed and to ensure their clients succeed. They approach every instruction with committed resolve.
