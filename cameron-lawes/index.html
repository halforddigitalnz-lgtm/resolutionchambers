Cameron Lawes is a founding member of Resolution Chambers. He is also a visiting university law lecturer at the AUT, delivering a new course he has written on civil and commercial litigation to final year law students.

Cameron is dual qualified in NZ and the UK, having spent 15 years in London handling high profile litigation cases across a range of subject matters. He now combines the skills and experience gained in both the British and New Zealand jurisdictions to deliver an exceptional litigation service.

Background

Cameron studied and trained in Wellington, where he worked as a criminal and commercial barrister and solicitor, originally learning his criminal craft under the late great Greg King.

Cameron then moved to the UK where he first worked for a High Court Judge at the Royal Courts of Justice in London. He later moved into private practice where he was a Partner for 10 years, rising to become a Senior Equity Partner, in the London office of a top international law firm.

As well as running a full time London litigation practice, Cameron also held dual management roles. He was Champion of the Real Estate Sector (accounting for 25% of total firm revenue) and Chairman of the firm’s Strategy Board (the senior board responsible for setting long term firm wide strategy).

Highlights during his time in practice in London included two significant cases that established precedents that changed the development of the law in the UK. Taking a case all the way to the House of Lords he developed a new approach to the way competition law impacts on the enforceability of contractual arrangements and in the High Court, his team established a new basis for an Administrator’s contractual liability where a company is insolvent. Both cases were heavily reported and founded a new basis for legal interpretation in the leading text books.

Cameron then returned to New Zealand, joined the Independent Bar, and established Resolution Chambers. Cameron maintains a nationwide legal practice accepting instructions in the Higher Courts throughout New Zealand.

Interests

Away from the office Cameron enjoys climbing mountains, running marathons and desert ultramarathons and collecting and racing motorcars. He has just started on his private pilot’s licence so looks forward to combining air travel with some of the activities above in the future.

In the last few years he has run self-supported multiday ultramarathons (250 km each) across the Sahara, Gobi, Atacama, Jordanian, Namibian, Simpson and Arizona deserts.

City breaks have included marathons in London, Berlin, Paris, Vienna, Venice, New York, Chicago, Cork, Loch Ness, and different towns in the UK and NZ – most often running them with his Mum.

He has also climbed many of the classic European alpine routes summiting Mt Blanc, the Matterhorn and many other European mountains. After 2 attempts on the Eiger (both abandoned for adverse weather) he dreams of returning to complete the classic trio.

While still dreaming of the European and Greater Ranges, following his return to NZ, Cameron has started his NZ mountaineering campaign. Having just summited Mt Aspiring (the Matterhorn of the South) and Mt Cook (NZ’s highest mountain) he looks forward to the other pleasures to be found in the Southern Alps.

Having grown up in Palmerston North, Cameron was not able to resist the draw of motoring. He maintains an (ever growing) collection of road cars. He also has a collection of racing cars both in NZ and the USA. He has gained a motorsport NZ racing drivers licence for both circuit and rally driving, and looks forward to more time on the circuits of NZ in his classic racing cars, and on the dirt and gravel roads of NZ in his classic rally car. He also acts for a significant and very successful motor racing client based in the USA competing in F3 and F4, which allows him to work in the motor racing sector too.

Cameron also dabbles in a bit of writing, having invented what he thinks is a new way of writing, which he has called ’scribing. You can view his published ’scribes on Substack.

Cameron is founder and CEO of The MARCH (The Multisport Adventure Racing Challenge) and he also writes business columns, accepts public speaking engagements, and writes children’s books.

With his wife he bought the previously closed Cable Bay Cafe, an idyllic cafe located in its own slice of Kiwi paradise, and built it up to rank as 8th most popular restaurant in the Nelson region out of 132 restaurants rated on Tripadvisor – now sold.

Cameron is blessed with a great family who support him in his endeavours and provide him with the greatest joy.
