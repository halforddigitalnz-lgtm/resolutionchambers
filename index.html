Our name

Resolution and Adventure in Matavai Bay, William Hodges, 1776. National Maritime Museum, Greenwich.

HMS Resolution was Captain Cook’s favourite ship. He described her as the fittest for service he had ever seen – a sentiment understood by the founding members of Resolution Chambers.

Along with HMS Adventure, both depicted in the painting above, Cook sailed her on his second and third voyages exploring the Pacific.
