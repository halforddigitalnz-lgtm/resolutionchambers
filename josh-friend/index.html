Background

Josh grew up in Nelson and attended Nelson College before moving to Dunedin to complete his tertiary education at the University of Otago. He graduated with a Bachelor of Laws (LLB) and a Bachelor of Arts, majoring in Politics and minoring in Economics.

After completing his studies, Josh returned to Nelson and was admitted as a Barrister and Solicitor of the High Court of New Zealand in May 2023.

Josh practises across a broad range of criminal matters, from driving offences through to serious violence, sexual and drug offending. He has appeared in both the High Court and Court of Appeal and has represented clients in defended Judge-alone and jury trials.

Josh is accredited to receive instructions through Legal Aid and is committed to providing clear, robust and practical advice. His approach is to carefully assess the evidence and circumstances of each case, identify the issues that matter, and work towards achieving the best outcome for his clients.

Josh understands that being involved in criminal proceedings can be a stressful and unfamiliar experience. He takes the time to ensure his clients understand the allegations they face, the procedural requirements of the criminal process, the available options and the likely consequences of each decision.

Interests

Outside of work, Josh enjoys spending time with friends and family. He also enjoys travelling throughout New Zealand and overseas whenever the opportunity arises. Sport and exercise are important to Josh.

Josh understands the value in being able to decompress. Reading, getting out into nature and the occasional session gaming are activities he uses to unwind.
