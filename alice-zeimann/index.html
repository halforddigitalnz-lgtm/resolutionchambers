Alice joined Resolution Chambers as a Barrister in late November 2024. Her practice is predominantly criminal law, alongside family law and youth advocacy. She provides practical and clear advice to clients at all stages of the criminal process.

Alice acts on a broad range of matters, including drink-driving, theft, assaults, family violence, and other summary offending. She also works alongside senior counsel, Michael Vesty, on complex and more serious criminal matters.

Alice is available to receive Legal Aid instructions.

Background

Alice holds an LLB from the University of Auckland and a Bachelor of Arts in Political Science from Florida State University.

Since joining Resolution Chambers, Alice has gained experience across a range of criminal matters, including allegations of sexual offending, kidnapping, assault, dangerous driving, drug possession/supply/importation and family violence.

Alice understands that appearing before the Court can be an overwhelming experience. She focuses on ensuring clients understand the process, receive clear advice, and have a strong advocate in their corner.

Interests

Outside of law, Alice has represented New Zealand as a professional volleyball and beach volleyball player for more than 10 years.

She was a collegiate volleyball and beach volleyball athlete at both the University of Minnesota and Florida State University, competing at the NCAA Division I level.

She competed at the 2022 Commonwealth Games and was part of New Zealand’s campaign towards the Paris 2024 Olympic Games.

Alice is also an advanced yellow belt with Seido Karate Nelson Shibu.

Her sporting background has developed the discipline, resilience and composure that she brings to her work as an advocate.
